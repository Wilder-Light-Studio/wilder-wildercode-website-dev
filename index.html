Serious tools for serious creatives.

WilderCode builds software for people who take their craft seriously — songwriters chasing the right rhyme, writers and editors who live at the keyboard, anyone whose work depends on precision, not novelty.

Inspired by nature and grounded in the state of the art. Each tool is built end to end, kept quiet and efficient under the hood, and designed to earn a permanent place in a real creative process rather than get tried once and forgotten.

How we build

Simple. Intuitive. Aesthetic.

01 Simple

The minimal complexity that solves the problem elegantly — never the terse version, just the uncluttered one.

02 Intuitive

The interface matches the mental model, so using it relies on understanding, not memorization — that's what actually saves time and conserves energy.

03 Aesthetic

Aesthetics matter. We hold to solid UI/UX principles across every design, so the tool aligns with the model — form following function, not fighting it.

Wilder Rhymes — Early Release

A rhyming dictionary built for songwriters and lyricists — search by sound, not spelling, with every match graded by closure, stress, and part of speech, based on Pat Pattison's Berklee rhyme methodology. It also surfaces word insight — definitions, synonyms, and connotation — in one place, replacing the dictionary and thesaurus tabs alongside it.
